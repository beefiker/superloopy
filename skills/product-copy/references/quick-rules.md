# Product Copy Quick Rules

Use this sequence for each rewrite or review.

## 1. Freeze supplied facts

Preserve product names, code, numbers, paths, URLs, quoted text, legal text, and user-supplied behavior. Do not paraphrase a protected value into a broader claim.

## 2. Find the reader's decision

Identify the smallest fact the reader needs:

- what happened;
- what remains;
- which fallback is active;
- what the product will do; or
- what the reader should do.

## 3. Check support clause by clause

Trace every behavioral clause in the proposed message to the source or explicit context. Delete an unsupported clause. A general safety or accuracy statement never supplies its mechanism.

## 4. Apply PC-1 through PC-5

- Remove vague safety and accuracy reassurance.
- Give failures one supplied outcome, retained state, fallback, recovery result, or next action.
- Preserve verified negative privacy and legal commitments; send them to manual review.
- Never invent recovery, encryption, retention, privacy, correctness, or safety behavior.
- Attach accuracy to a measurement, specification, identity, or match. Replace `정확한 컴퓨터`-style entity labels with the supplied relation; keep measurable phrases such as `정확한 사양`.

## 5. Choose one output

- Sufficient facts: return only the smallest complete rewrite at the source register.
- Missing required behavior: return one precise question naming the missing fact, with no draft or alternatives.
- File-backed work: store source, final, summary, and audit JSON, then run the packaged audit script.
